# Trademarks and Brand Guidelines

This document outlines the policy regarding the use of the name and logo of **IdleShift**.

> **Note**: This document is not formal legal advice. It clarifies community expectations and trademark boundaries.

## 1. The Name "IdleShift"

- The name "IdleShift" and any associated logos or icons represent the official project and releases.
- You **may not** use the name "IdleShift" in a manner that falsely implies sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation with, or origination from the official IdleShift project or maintainers.

## 2. Modified Versions and Forks

- If you distribute a modified version, fork, or derivative work of this software, you **must distribute it under a distinctly different name**.
- You may not use "IdleShift" as the primary name or brand of your fork (e.g., calling a fork "IdleShift Pro" or "IdleShift Enhanced" without explicit prior permission is prohibited).
- You may use a factual explanatory statement to describe the origin, such as:
  - *"Based on IdleShift open source code"*
  - *"Forked from the IdleShift project"*

## 3. Factual and Nominative Use

- Factual references to the project name in documentation, academic papers, articles, tutorials, or comparison discussions are fully permitted (e.g., *"IdleShift is an MCP-based blackboard system for Windows"*).
- Nominative fair use of the trademark to describe interoperability or compatibility is permitted (e.g., *"Compatible with IdleShift MCP protocol"*).

## 4. Other Trademarks

- All other trademarks, product names, logos, and company names mentioned in this repository (such as Claude, Codex, Antigravity, Windows, Microsoft, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) are the property of their respective owners. Their mention does not imply endorsement, affiliation, or sponsorship.
